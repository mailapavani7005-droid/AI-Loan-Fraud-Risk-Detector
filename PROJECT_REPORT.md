# AI Loan Fraud Risk Detector

## Objective
To identify risky loan applicants using AI-based risk assessment.

## Problem
Banks process many loan applications daily. Manual verification is slow and may miss risky applicants.

## Solution
The system analyzes applicant information such as income, credit score, and loan amount to classify applicants as Low Risk, Medium Risk, or High Risk.

## Benefits
- Faster loan approval process
- Reduced fraud risk
- Better decision making

## Future Scope
- Integration with real banking systems
- Machine learning-based prediction
- Real-time fraud monitoring
